# Career Income Optimization

## Purpose

Maximize earning power with the least additional work by focusing study and projects on the shortest path from the current resume to high-value roles.

This epic is the strategy layer above the job search:

- it identifies the most valuable skill gaps
- it ranks the best roles to pursue first
- it turns gaps into study topics and portfolio projects
- it avoids wasting time on high-effort pivots with low return

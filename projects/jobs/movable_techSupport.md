## Gap Closure Plan

1. Study the exact role stack.
   - REST vs GraphQL basics
   - OAuth, SAML, and SCIM concepts
   - browser debugging terms: HAR, XMLHttpRequest, page source, iframe behavior
   - cloud-hosting vocabulary and common support tools

2. Build one concrete support story for each weak area.
   - a web-app issue reproduction story
   - an auth/debugging story
   - a documentation or knowledge-base improvement story
   - an escalation-to-engineering story

3. Tighten the resume language.
   - emphasize incident resolution, triage, and documentation
   - keep support ownership and root-cause language near the top
   - avoid claiming SaaS depth you do not have yet

4. Prepare interview answers.
   - how you troubleshoot a customer issue from first contact to resolution
   - how you handle an unknown technology stack
   - how you document and hand off unresolved bugs
   - how you work on-call without losing process discipline

5. Decide if the role is a target or maybe.
   - if the prep above feels manageable, keep it as a target
   - if the stack remains too far off, keep it as a strong maybe and prioritize adjacent support roles with less SaaS depth
